# Expanded 14-scene MiniMax audio

These 14 source WAV files were generated with MiniMax `speech-2.8-hd`, voice
`Tender CEO`, and assigned to scenes by the Downloads file modification time
(ascending). The original downloaded filenames, timestamps, durations, and
scene mapping are recorded in `manifest.json`.

The separate `opening.wav` is the title/author announcement. Its original
download was moved out of Downloads into this archive after import; it is not
part of the numbered scene sequence.

The board-ready copies are in `../../odyssey_homecoming/audio/` and use
16 kHz/16-bit/mono PCM WAV. No legacy six-scene audio is mixed into this set.
